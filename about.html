About Us

Welcome to North Star Bakery! We are a family-owned business dedicated to creating the finest baked goods in the community. Since 1995, we have been using traditional recipes and the highest quality ingredients to bring joy to our customers every day.
Our team of skilled bakers and pastry chefs work tirelessly to ensure that every loaf of bread, cookie, and pastry is made with love and care. We take pride in our commitment to excellence and our passion for baking.

Our Ingredients

We ensure our ingredients are locally sourced and organic whenever possible. We believe in supporting our local farmers and suppliers, and we strive to minimize our environmental impact through sustainable practices.
Thank you for choosing North Star Bakery. We look forward to serving you and sharing our love!
