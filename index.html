Welcome to the North Star Bakery where we bake just like grandma does!

What we are known for

We are a local bakery in your hometown that is known for our locally made delicious pastries and bread. We make all of our baked goods from scratch each morning and bake throughout the day to make sure you get fresh bread or pastry each time you visit.
We are best known for our fresh bread and cookies and also our catering events!

Hours and Location

We are located at 123 Main Street, Hometown, USA.
We are open Monday through Friday from 7am to 6pm and Saturday from 8am to 4pm. We are closed on Sundays.
